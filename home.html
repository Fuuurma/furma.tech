The Studio

About
Furma.tech

Furma.tech is a 100% bootstrapped digital venture studio based in Sitges, Catalonia, operating as an Estonian OÜ. We build, own, and operate every product we ship.

No venture capital. No burn rate anxiety. The business model is simple: industry SaaS generates steady MRR that funds AI R&D. Everything compounds.

We build in public, document everything, and operate with a strict constraint: if it can't sustain itself, it doesn't get built.

01
Bootstrapped forever

0% VC. 100% retained earnings. Profits are reinvested into infrastructure and product — never extracted early.

02
Sovereign by design

Every Aitlas product runs on user-owned keys and self-hosted compute. We sell tools, not lock-in.

03
Standards over proprietary

MCP-first, polyrepo architecture, open schemas. Built to last and built to interoperate.

04
Profitable, not famous

Metrics that matter: MRR, churn, and compute margin. Not GitHub stars or press coverage.
