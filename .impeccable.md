## Design Context

### Users
Investors and B2B clients evaluating Furma.tech's portfolio and credibility. They visit to understand the studio's range (11 products across games, SaaS, AI, finance, civic tech) and assess competence. Context: professional evaluation, often on desktop during work hours. The site is a portfolio showcase — not a conversion funnel or product app.

### Brand Personality
**Quiet, refined, confident.** The studio speaks through restraint and precision, not volume. Every element earns its place. The aesthetic signals competence without needing to prove it. Think editorial typography, intentional whitespace, and a monochrome palette that trusts the content over decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Restrained minimal with editorial gravitas. Inspired by Linear and Vercel — clean, technical, premium.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode. Default light. Monochrome palette with zero chroma (pure black/white in oklch) is intentional — this is a brand choice, not an oversight.
- **Typography**: Syne for headings (geometric, assertive), Cormorant for editorial accents (high contrast serif), JetBrains Mono for technical details. Sharp hierarchy through size contrast, not decoration.
- **References**: Linear (dark-mode polish, spatial rhythm), Vercel (type-forward, editorial confidence)
- **Anti-references**: Generic startup landing pages, colorful/playful palettes, AI-generated aesthetics (purple gradients, glassmorphism overload, template card grids)

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over spectacle** — If in doubt, remove it. White space is a feature, not waste.
2. **Content-first hierarchy** — Typography and spacing do the work. No decorative gradients, no border-left accents, no gradient text.
3. **Monochrome confidence** — Zero chroma is the brand. No accent colors needed. Contrast comes from weight, size, and spacing alone.
4. **Editorial rhythm** — Asymmetric layouts, varied spacing, intentional asymmetry. Not everything centered or gridded identically.
5. **Production-grade craft** — Every interaction responsive, every transition purposeful. Motion should feel instant and springy, never slow or decorative.
